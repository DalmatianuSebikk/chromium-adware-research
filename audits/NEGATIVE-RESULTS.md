## What the negatives add up to

- Chrome's ad-reachable decoding and media paths are **fuzzed and audited to a level** that a solo effort with targeted methods doesn't crack by breadth.
- The DCHECK-oracle method (entries 4–5) works — it just needs **pointed inputs**, not random ones: every invariant we probed needed a compiler-pipeline-specific shape to have any chance.
- Entry 7 is the most instructive: an upstream-tracked crash class, a fully ad-deliverable surface, an engine that does everything the crashes imply — and the release instrumentation never fires. The armor below the surface is real.
- Entry 9 is the other half of the discipline: a finding can survive line-level verification and still be wrong, because *composing* separately-true edges is where the error lives. Every "verified chain" claim here gets a second pass before it's filed anywhere.
- Entries 10–15 close the image family completely: the C++ survivors (JPEG, WebP) are audited, and everything else moved to memory-safe languages (wuffs, Rust). The 2008-era image-attack surface is gone from Chrome at this revision — that's a fact we paid ~6 full audits to establish, and it's now on record.
- Entry 18 shows the registry working as designed: the refutation discriminator was built *before* claiming a leak, and it killed the hypothesis with evidence (allocator scribble beats controlled inheritance). A wrong "info leak" claim would have wasted a VRP reviewer's time and ours.
- Entry 19 is the sharpest strategic fact in the file: the HarfBuzz delta is being actively mined by automated scanners. Whoever audits remaining C++ cones should assume the clock is running.
- Entries 20–24 close the second wave: the media-control and process-swap machinery holds, the two upstream HB memory bugs that *looked* Chrome-relevant are dead on reachability (entry 22 — and the pin match means our audits describe shipped Chrome exactly), and the chunked harness (entry 24) turns the "fuzzers never stream" gap into a repeatable experiment that came back clean on first contact.

## Reproducing any entry

- Entries 4–5: build V8 with `is_debug=false dcheck_always_on=true v8_enable_slow_dchecks=true`, replay any corpus with the flags listed in the stress scripts.
- Entry 6: Fuzzilli with the V8 profile, `--storagePath` corpus, jobs scaled to the machine.
- Entry 7: run the PoC (see its ELI5) and watch the renderer console.
- Entries 10–17: the audit docs in this directory carry the file-level detail; all were read at the pinned revision in a full or targeted line pass, with the consumer/call-graph verified before auditing (e.g., GIF's C++ reader was confirmed *gone* before auditing wuffs instead).
